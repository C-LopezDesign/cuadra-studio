Privacy

Updated October 17, 2026

Cuadra Studio is a one person web design studio in Los Angeles. This page explains what this website collects and why. It is short because there is not much.

Analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand how people use this site: which pages are visited, where visitors come from, and where they click and scroll. Clarity also creates session replays and heatmaps. Both tools use cookies. Nothing is used for advertising, and we do not sell data.

If you decline

If you decline, analytics cookies are not set. The tools may still count basic, cookieless visits. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer of the home page.

Contact form

If you send a message through the contact form, we receive your name, email, and anything else you write. The message is delivered by a form service called Web3Forms. We use it to reply to you about your project.

Other services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It loads fonts from Google Fonts. On devices with a mouse, the Clients section loads preview screenshots from WordPress mShots. Like any website request, these send your IP address to the provider.

Questions

To ask about data connected to a message you sent, reach out through the contact form. If this page changes, the date above will change too.
